## Overview

Pawn'd Chess is a chess trainer designed to meet players at their current skill level.

Instead of relying on traditional engine behavior that can feel overly precise or punishing, Pawn'd Chess uses human-like Maia opponents from roughly 600 to 2600 Elo.

Players can choose an opponent that matches their ability, practice without pressure, track local rating progression, and return to positions they want to study.

The game is designed around controller-first navigation and Steam Deck play, with accessibility considered throughout the interface.

## Training and Position Tools

Pawn'd Chess allows players to create, load, save, and revisit positions they care about.

Players can use saved positions to practice openings, middlegames, endgames, tactical situations, or positions from real games.

Examples include:

- London System
- Queen's Gambit
- Sicilian Defense
- Caro-Kann
- King's Indian Defense
- Endgame practice

Any position represented by a valid FEN can be loaded and played from that point.

## Practice Mode

Practice Mode allows players to:

- undo moves
- redo moves
- test alternative ideas
- revisit mistakes
- experiment with different plans
- play from saved or custom positions

The goal is to make experimentation part of the learning process rather than treating every move as permanent.

## Steam Deck and Controller Support

Pawn'd Chess is designed with controller-first navigation and Steam Deck use in mind.

The interface is built to remain readable and usable at Steam Deck resolutions, with controller navigation supported throughout the experience.

## Accessibility

Accessibility is considered throughout the game, including interface readability, visual options, controller navigation, and configurable presentation.

## Local Multiplayer

Pawn'd Chess includes local hotseat multiplayer, allowing two players to play classic chess against each other on one device.
